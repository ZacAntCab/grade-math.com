How It Works

If you've ever been worried about your grades, and what your GPA will be after this semester this calculator is perfect for you. It's an easy and simple way to find what grade you need to keep your GPA up. Here's how it works:

  • 1

    Input Your Current GPA

    In the first input box labeled "Current GPA", type what your GPA is before the current semester.

  • 2

    Enter Your Total Credits Earned

    In the "Total Credits Earned" box, input the amount of credits you have earned before this semester.

  • 3

    Enter Your Target GPA

    In the third input field, type the GPA you would like to achieve.

  • 4

    Enter Your Remaining Credits

    Enter the total amount of credits your remaining courses are worth.

  • 4

    Let the Calculator Do It's Magic

How to Calculate Your Grade

Now that you know how to use this calculator, you might want to know how to do these calculations by yourself. Here's how to calculate your needed GPA on your own:

  • 1

    Multiply Your Current GPA by Your Credits Earned


    e.g.  3.2 * 12 = 38.4

  • 2

    Add Your Credits Earned and Your Remaining Credits


    e.g.  12 + 5 = 17

  • 3

    Multiply Your Target GPA by Step 2

    Take the results from Step 2 and multiply your desired GPA by this number

    e.g.  3.4 * 17 = 57.8

  • 4

    Subtract Step 1 from Step 3

    Take the results from Step 3 and subtract the number you got from Step 1.

    e.g.  57.8 - 38.4 = 19.4

  • 5

    Divide Step 4 by Your Remaining Credits

    To do this, use the number you got from Step 4 and divide it by your remaining credits.

    e.g.  19.4 / 5 = 3.9

  • 6

    You're All Done

    Now just make sure you get that grades you need in order to get the GPA you want.
