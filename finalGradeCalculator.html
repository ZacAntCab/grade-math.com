How It Works

Ever wondered what grade you're going to need on your final exam to get the grade you want? Well than this calculator is perfect for you, simple and straight to the point. Here's how to use it:

  • 1

    Enter Your Current Grade

    Enter the grade you have in your class before taking your final exam.

  • 2

    Enter Your Target Grade

    Enter the grade you would like to have in your class after taking the final exam.

  • 3

    Enter the Weight of Your Final Exam

    Enter the percent of your class grade that the final exam will be worth. To find out what this is you can either ask your teacher or find it on the class syllabus.

    Let the Calculator Do It's Magic

How to Calculate the Final Grade You Need

Now that you know how to use this calculator, you might want to know how to do these calculations by yourself. Here's how to calculate your needed final grade on your own:

  • 1

    Find the Weight of Your Current Grade

    For this you will need to subtract your final grade weight from 100%.

    e.g.  100% - 30% = 70%

  • 2

    Multiply Your Current Grade by Its Weight

    To do this, take the results from Step 1 and multiply your current grade by it

    e.g.  88 * 70% = 61.6

  • 3

    Subtract From Target Grade

    For this you will need to subtract the results of Step 2 from your desired grade.

    e.g.  90 - 61.6 = 28.4

  • 4

    Divide By Final Exam Weight

    Divide the results of Step 3 by the weight of your final exam.

    e.g.  28.4 / 30% = 94.67

  • 4

    Study Hard Enough to Get the Grade You Want

    Now that you know what grade you need to get the grade you want, make sure you put in enough effort to get the final exam grade you need.
