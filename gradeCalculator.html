How It Works

At some point in your schooling journey you've probably wondered what grade your going to achieve once the end of the semester or year comes around, and if this is the case then this calculator is perfect for you. Here's how it works:

  • 1

    Add Grades

    Add or remove grades until you have the amount of grades you are going to be calculating the average grade for.

  • 2

    Enter Each Grade's Name

    Although it is not necessary, it might make it much easier to keep track of all the different grades you are inputing, by entering the name of the assignment in the grade name input.

  • 3

    Enter Each Grade

    In the grade input enter each grade as a whole number between 0 and 100.

  • 4

    Enter Each Grade's Weight

    In the weight input enter each grade's weight, which can be figured out by checking the course's syllabus or asking your teacher. For example, each homework assignment might be worth 20% of your grade so for each homework assigment you would enter "20" as the weight input.

  • 4

    Let the Calculator Do It's Magic

How to Calculate Your Grade

Now that you know how to use this calculator, you might want to know how to do these calculations by yourself. Here's how to calculate your grade average on your own:

  • 1

    Multiply Each Grade by Its Weight

    To do this take the grade from each of your assignments and multiply them by their weights in percentage form, or as each weight divided by 100.

    e.g.  96 * 40% = 38.4
    e.g.  84 * 40% = 33.6
    e.g.  91 * 60% = 54.6

  • 2

    Add Together the Converted Grades

    Once you have multiplied each grade by its weight, add those results together.

    e.g.  38.4 + 33.6 + 54.6 = 126.6

  • 3

    Divide 100 by Sum of Weights

    Divide the number 100, by the sum of all the weights for the grades

    e.g.  100 / 140 = 0.714

  • 4

    Multiply Step 2 and Step 3

    Take the results from Step 2 and multiply that number by the decimal from Step 3.

    e.g.  126.6 * 0.714 = 90.4

  • 5

    You're All Done

    Now just keep up the good grades to make sure you end with the grade you want.
